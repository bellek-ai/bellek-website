The App

How Pollcy works

Pollcy automatically generates polls from real news stories and surfaces the results in ways that are actually meaningful.

📰

News-driven polls

Polls are generated daily from trending headlines so you're always voting on what matters right now.

📊

Demographic breakdown

See how age, location, and other demographics shape opinion — not just the overall vote count.

⚡

Live results

Vote and watch results update in real time as more people weigh in throughout the day.

🔒

Anonymous & private

Your individual votes are anonymized after the voting window closes. No one can see how you voted.

About Bellek

Bellek is a California-based software company focused on building products that help people engage with the world around them. Pollcy is our first mobile application, available on iOS.

We believe that understanding how others think — across backgrounds and demographics — makes for better conversations and more informed opinions.
